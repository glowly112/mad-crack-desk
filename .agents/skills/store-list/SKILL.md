Use when they talk listing or ASO. After store-research and store-fit. Then [ASO hammer](sand-workflow:aso-hammer) must pass before anyone says done. en-GB unless they say otherwise.

Load live listing limits and Review 2.3 from [developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/) this turn. Wiki numbers are hints.

## 1. What it is

Restate the job in one line from the product (brief, screens, locks). Not from a cool name.

Write: it is X. It is not Y.

Name, subtitle, keywords, and the first screenshot must match that line. If a candidate name could be wallpaper, a sketch studio, a notes IDE, a typewriter toy, or an AI writer, drop it.

Prefer **one word** only when that word still says the job and is search-clear. Two words if the one word is brand-only or already owned in search.

Live-check the exact title and the first token on iTunes GB + US this turn. Title-clear is not search-clear.

When they lock a listing name, set the home-screen display name (`CFBundleDisplayName`) in the **same** ship. Listing-only is not the name change.

## 2. Indexed (160 characters)

| Field | Limit | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | 30 | Says what it is. Distinctive. Easy to spell. Not too similar to a live app. |
| Subtitle | 30 | The rest of the job. Not a rescue for a confused name. |
| Keywords | 100 | Hidden. Comma-separated, no spaces after commas. |

Do not repeat a word already in the name or subtitle inside keywords. Do not put competitor names, category names, “app”, or a plural of a word already listed. Description and promo text are **not** indexed. Do not stuff them.

## 3. Listing

- Description for humans. First sentence is the only line most people read. It repeats the job line.
- Screenshot story: open → type → result. First shot is the search card of that job.
- Screenshots are the app in use, not title art.
- Privacy URL and support URL before Review.

## Don’t

- Don’t invent ranks or download volumes.
- Don’t install aso-skill, ASOManiac, Appeeky, or Search Ads rank trackers.
- Don’t submit App Review from this skill.
- Don’t claim an ipa left this computer.
- Don’t leave the home-screen name on the old title after they locked a new one.
